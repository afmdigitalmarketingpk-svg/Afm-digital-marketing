    FAQs

    Frequently asked questions

    Do you provide media buyers?

    Yes. Dedicated media buyers plan, launch and optimize your campaigns.

    Can you create my Facebook Business Manager?

    Yes. We create and set up Business Manager, Ad Account, Page connection and pixel.

    Is the ad budget included in your fee?

    No. The ad budget paid to Meta/TikTok and AFM’s management fee are separate.

    Do you guarantee sales or leads?

    No honest agency can. We commit to clear tracking, weekly optimization and transparent reports.

    How much budget do I need?

    It depends on your industry, goal and market. Use the AI Ads Consultant or budget calculator for an estimate.

    How long until I see results?

    Setup takes about 3–5 days, then a 7-day learning phase. A clearer picture usually appears after 2–3 weeks.

    Which platforms do you cover?

    Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ads, including Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns.
